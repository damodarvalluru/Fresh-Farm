# Fish photo credits

The photographs in this folder are real-world, species-accurate images sourced
from Wikimedia Commons so customers can see the actual fish they are buying.
They are stored locally to keep the storefront independent of third-party image
hosts. Each file is a 960 px JPEG thumbnail.

The commerce data (names, prices, stock, descriptions) is maintained in
`backend/database/seed.sql`. No cooked dishes or prepared foods are represented here:
the catalogue is limited to live fish, whole fresh fish and raw cuts.
